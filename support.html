Support

Bug reports, questions and wishes are welcome.

From the app

Go to Settings, About, Bugs and wishes. The app shows exactly what will be sent, and only sends it when you press send in your own mail app.

By mail

pocketssh.feedback@proton.me

Never send private keys, passwords or the addresses of your servers. They are never needed to get help.

Purchases

Purchases are made through Google Play and follow your Google account. If you have a new phone, tap Restore purchases under Settings, Pro and trial. Refunds are handled by Google Play.
