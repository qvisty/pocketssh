Privacy policy for Pocket SSH Terminal

Effective from 21 October 2026.

In short

Pocket SSH Terminal sends no information about you to us or to anyone else. Everything you create in the app stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

The following is stored on your phone only:

  • Server profiles, that is name, address, port and user name.
  • SSH keys. The private part is encrypted with a key in Android's secure key store and can only be used after fingerprint or screen lock.
  • Passwords, but only if you choose to remember them. They are encrypted exactly like the SSH keys.
  • Accepted host keys for your servers.
  • Favorite commands and settings.
  • A local event log of connections and host keys. It never contains commands or output.

Terminal content, history and search exist only in memory while the app is running.

The app is excluded from Android backup and from transfer to a new phone, so none of the above ends up in a cloud. When you uninstall the app, all of it is deleted.

What leaves the phone

  • The SSH connection to the server you chose yourself. It is encrypted, and we have no access to it.
  • Bugs and wishes. Only if you choose to write to us. The app shows exactly what will be sent, and nothing is sent until you tap send. The message is sent encrypted to Q Digital's receiver at qdigital.dk, hosted by Simply.com in Denmark, and on to us as a mail. It only contains your text, your email if you write it, and the app version, Android version and phone model if you choose so. If sending fails, you can send it with your own mail app instead. We use it to reply to you and to improve the app, we do not share it with anyone, and we delete it if you ask us to.
  • Three questions for an extra week. Only if you choose it when the free week is over. The answers are sent in the same way as Bugs and wishes, without an email. They do not go to Google Play and have nothing to do with a rating in the store.
  • Ratings. In the Play edition, Google Play may show its own box for rating the app. Google controls it, and the app never learns whether or how you rate it.
  • Purchases. Purchases are handled by Google Play. Google processes the payment and the related information under Google's own privacy policy. The app only learns whether Pro has been bought.

What the app does not do

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting and no account with us.
